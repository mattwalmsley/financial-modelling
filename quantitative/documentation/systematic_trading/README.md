# Systematic Trading

A comprehensive guide to systematic trading, covering strategy development, statistical foundations, risk management, portfolio construction, and execution.

## Contents

1. [Introduction](01_introduction.md) — What is systematic trading, strategy classifications, core examples, common mistakes, and development workflow.
2. [Statistical Foundations & Market Theory](02_statistical_foundations.md) — Return distributions, Sharpe Ratio, EMH, CAPM, APT, behavioural finance, and sources of return.
3. [Uncertainty & Prediction](03_uncertainty_and_prediction.md) — Types of uncertainty, prediction from past data, conditional vs unconditional forecasts, and predicting means.
4. [Predicting Risk & Returns](04_predicting_risk_and_returns.md) — Volatility estimation, Sharpe Ratio prediction, exponential weighting, full distribution prediction, and correlations.
5. [Relative Performance](05_relative_performance.md) — Comparing paired returns, variance of mean differences, benchmarking and regression, backtesting vs live trading.
6. [Fitting & Optimisation](06_fitting_and_optimisation.md) — Strategy creation, EWMAC example, degrees of freedom, in-sample/out-of-sample methods, optimisation techniques, and pooling.
7. [Position Sizing, Leverage & Risk Targeting](07_position_sizing.md) — Volatility-based sizing, geometric means, Kelly criterion, conviction, and margin constraints.
8. [Measuring & Managing Risk](08_risk_management.md) — Risk types, standard deviation, correlation effects, VaR, Expected Shortfall, and other risk measures.
9. [Portfolio Construction](09_portfolio_construction.md) — Fama-French portfolios, inverse volatility weighting, risk targeting, exposure controls, and relative value warnings.
10. [Portfolio Optimisation](10_portfolio_optimisation.md) — Markowitz optimisation, robustness problems, parameter uncertainty effects, and solutions.
11. [Trading Speed, Execution & Costs](11_trading_costs.md) — Trading speed vs returns, cost measurement, order book mechanics, cost normalisation, and cost reduction.
